03 · Product boundary

Specific capabilities are stated only when confirmed.

The public positioning is intentionally disciplined. The confirmed scope is ERP accounting and automated bookkeeping. Detailed claims about modules, integrations, tax workflows, payroll, inventory or other functions should be published only when their shipped scope is confirmed.

Stream is not Velocity.

Velocity serves market research and historical financial intelligence. Stream serves the operating company’s accounting environment. Keeping those jobs distinct makes the product easier to understand and keeps customer expectations precise.

04 · Ecosystem

A business-operations layer inside SARKSH GROW.

Stream sits beside Velocity—not beneath it. The two products serve different customers and can share institutional trust without blurring their product responsibilities.

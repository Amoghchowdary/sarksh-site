05 · Answers

Understand SARKSH before the first conversation.

Short answers to the questions visitors should not have to search for.

What is SARKSH GROW?

SARKSH GROW is a finance-first FinTech institution. The public product ecosystem is SARKSH Velocity for financial intelligence, SARKSH Stream for ERP accounting and bookkeeping, and SARKSH Trade as the long-term brokerage and trading-services platform direction.

What is SARKSH Velocity?

SARKSH Velocity is a financial-data and market-intelligence product that combines company information, disclosures, financial statements, charts, news and preserved historical analysis in a configurable research environment.

What is SARKSH Stream?

SARKSH Stream is a developed, customer-ready ERP, accounting and automated bookkeeping product for business-finance operations.

Does SARKSH GROW provide guaranteed returns or stock tips?

No. SARKSH GROW does not provide guaranteed returns, and the website does not publish promises of fixed returns or personalised stock tips.

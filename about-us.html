02 · Principles

Clarity is part of the operating model.

Financial products carry obligations. SARKSH GROW’s public communication should distinguish what exists, what is optional and what remains a future regulated direction.

01

State what is available

Current, optional and future capabilities are separated instead of presented as one undifferentiated feature list.

02

Preserve context

Financial information should remain traceable and distinguishable from statistical or AI interpretation.

03

No guaranteed outcomes

Historical information, research and AI-assisted analysis are not presented as guarantees of investment performance.

04

Customer boundaries

Research, business software and future execution maintain distinct customer responsibilities and compliance boundaries.

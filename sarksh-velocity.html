01 · Core capability

Financial context, assembled in one environment.

Velocity is designed to reduce the work of reconstructing a listed company’s recent history across disconnected screeners, exchange pages, charts and news sources.

01

Company intelligence

Fundamentals, financial statements, commonly used ratios, corporate announcements, exchange disclosures, company developments and relevant news.

02

Historical intelligence

Quarter-oriented company context and day-by-day stock analysis preserving chart states, candles, trendlines, volume, momentum and dated market observations.

03

Configurable terminal

Users can combine charts, filings, news, ratios, technical views, datasets and historical records around the company or workflow they are researching.

Eight layers of the Velocity proposition.

01

Unified intelligence terminal

03 · Historical layer

The past, preserved as an analytical record.

Velocity is designed to preserve more than historical price. A dated record can connect chart imagery and pattern observations with company information, filings, news and the market behavior around those events.

Research starts with context—not a blank screen.

The current product descriptions frame company intelligence around an approximately 90-day / quarter-oriented context and stock-side day-by-day analysis around roughly 40 days. These windows describe the research model and should be read as product framing rather than a guarantee of universal coverage.

04 · Workflow

From filing to structured research.

For permitted exchange documents, the intended workflow is to extract material facts, compare relevant information with authoritative records and present the result through concise notes, tables, charts or supporting visuals.

01

Select

Choose a listed company or a research question.

02

Assemble

Bring company data, historical charts, disclosures and news into one workspace.

03

Verify

Compare material facts across source and validation channels where feasible.

04

Interpret

Use the assembled context as an input to independent judgment, not as guaranteed prediction.

06 · Trust boundary

Research infrastructure, not a promise of returns.

Historical patterns, AI-assisted analysis and analytical observations do not guarantee future performance. Future personalized recommendations, order routing or broker-connected execution require the applicable legal, technical and exchange framework.

Facts, calculations and AI interpretation remain distinguishable.

Velocity’s product direction emphasizes source provenance, cross-verification and clear separation between sourced records, statistical findings and AI-assisted interpretation. That distinction is part of the product’s trust architecture.

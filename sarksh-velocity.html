Historical intelligence is the core layer

Velocity is designed to preserve an analytical time series, not merely a price chart. A dated record can connect chart imagery and pattern observations with company filings, news and the market behavior that followed. The product descriptions currently frame company intelligence around a rolling-quarter context and stock-side day-by-day analysis around roughly 40 days.

The purpose is not to predict certainty. It is to reduce the manual work required to reconstruct what happened, what information was available, and how the market behaved around it.

Filing and document intelligence

For a permitted NSE or BSE corporate filing, the intended workflow is to ingest the source document, extract figures, dates and material facts, identify business events, compare relevant facts with authoritative records, produce an accessible summary and present the result through key notes, tables, charts or supporting visuals.

Interpretation boundary: A favorable announcement is not automatically a positive trade signal. Valuation, expectations, timing, liquidity and whether information is already reflected in the market can change the outcome.

How a customer uses Velocity

A trader or investor can select a listed company, open a company summary, review financial ratios and filings, inspect recent price history and dated chart states, add relevant headlines, and request a concise report. The assembled context is then used as an input to the customer’s own decision-making process.

Quality, speed and trust

Velocity separates sourced facts, statistical findings and AI interpretation. Product observations reported during MVP work include filing summarization in approximately 5–8 minutes and some analytical workflows in approximately 10–15 minutes; these are early observations rather than universal service-level guarantees.

Historical patterns, AI-generated analysis and analytical observations do not guarantee future performance. Any future order routing, personalized recommendation or broker-connected execution must operate within the applicable legal, technical and exchange framework.

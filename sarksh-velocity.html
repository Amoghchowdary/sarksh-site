SARKSH Velocity

SARKSH Velocity is the SARKSH Data market-intelligence product. It organizes large volumes of historical company and stock-market information into graphs, tables, images and summarized analytical documents for faster historical understanding.

Key points

Historical intelligence

Historical research trail

Velocity is designed to preserve an organized view of company information, market behavior, charts, patterns, trendlines, news and announcements across a recent historical period.

Cross-verification

Multiple-source validation

Important facts are intended to be compared across multiple sources and validation channels where feasible before they are included in the research output.

Decision context

User-led decisions

The product provides historical context. It does not imply guaranteed returns, guaranteed profitability or certainty about future price movement.

What a Velocity report contains

Company intelligence

Company and financial context

Financial statements, commonly used ratios, corporate announcements, business developments, news and historical company context can be organized into summaries, tables, graphs and visual documents.

Historical view

Company and stock history

The product description outlines an approximately 90-day company-history view and an approximately 40-day stock-history view with day-by-day charts, candlesticks, trendlines, volume and momentum context.

Research compression

Structured reports

Large volumes of source material are compressed into readable reports so a user can review the historical context without manually reconstructing it from many separate websites, filings and charts.

How the product is intended to be used

A user selects a company, opens its prepared historical intelligence package, reviews the company-information layer and stock-history layer, inspects the visual chronology, and uses that historical context as an input to their own decision-making process.

The intended distinction is the historical research trail: what the chart looked like, what patterns and trendlines were observable, what company information was available, and what news or announcements occurred during the period.

Cross-verification and traceability

Velocity is designed to compare information across multiple sources and validation channels where feasible instead of treating a single source or AI output as unquestioned truth. Production use should also consider source reliability, timestamp integrity, licensing or usage rights and traceability.

01 · Direction

The future transaction layer.

SARKSH Trade is the company’s long-term brokerage and trading-services direction. The concept is to connect market access with research, account workflows and customer-controlled execution inside the wider SARKSH ecosystem.

01

Trading account & execution

A trading and execution experience would be made available only after the relevant legal, exchange, registration and operational prerequisites are satisfied.

02

Velocity context

The future platform may connect SARKSH Velocity research and historical intelligence with a trading workflow while keeping customer permissions explicit.

03

Integrated records

Account, transaction and reporting experiences may be connected where technically and legally permissible.

02 · Regulatory boundary

Future market access is not presented as current stockbroking.

Brokerage registration, exchange membership, broker technology/API arrangements, algorithm-related permissions and operational risk systems require the applicable review and approvals. The website does not treat a future product direction as an already licensed service.

Customer control remains central.

AI-generated analysis, scenarios or research context do not guarantee performance and do not remove customer responsibility for authorized actions. Any regulated execution layer must operate within the applicable framework.

03 · Ecosystem

Research first. Operations second. Transactions when permitted.

Velocity is the flagship financial-intelligence layer. Stream is the current business-software layer. Trade is the future transaction layer.
